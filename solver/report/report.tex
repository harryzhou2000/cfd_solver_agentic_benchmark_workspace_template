\begin{abstract}
SaturnCFD is an original C++17/MPI cell-centred unstructured solver for the two-dimensional compressible Euler and laminar Navier--Stokes equations. It combines CGNS multi-zone topology, METIS graph partitioning, neighbour-only halos, limited second-order reconstruction, a Rusanov flux, Newton--Fourier viscous terms, 4-by-4 block implicit defect correction, and frozen-history BDF2. The run-status table identifies the actual completion status for every required case; every numerical claim and figure in this report is regenerated from the submitted CSV and parallel VTK files.
\end{abstract}
\section{Governing equations and nondimensionalization}
The conservative state is $\mathbf U=[\rho,\rho u,\rho v,\rho E]^T$ and the solved system is
\[\partial_t\mathbf U+\partial_x\mathbf F^i+\partial_y\mathbf G^i=\partial_x\mathbf F^v+\partial_y\mathbf G^v.\]
The inviscid fluxes are
\[\mathbf F^i=[\rho u,\rho u^2+p,\rho uv,u(\rho E+p)]^T,\quad\mathbf G^i=[\rho v,\rho uv,\rho v^2+p,v(\rho E+p)]^T.\]
A calorically perfect gas closes the system: $p=(\gamma-1)\rho e$, $E=e+(u^2+v^2)/2$, and $a=\sqrt{\gamma p/\rho}$, with $\gamma=1.4$ and $R=1$. Reference values are $\rho_\infty=U_\infty=L=A=1$, $p$ is scaled by $\rho_\infty U_\infty^2$, and force coefficients use $q_\infty A$ with $q_\infty=\rho_\infty U_\infty^2/2$.
For laminar cases, $\mu=\rho_\infty U_\infty L_{Re}/Re$, $\tau_{xx}=2\mu u_x-2\mu(u_x+v_y)/3$, $\tau_{yy}=2\mu v_y-2\mu(u_x+v_y)/3$, $\tau_{xy}=\mu(u_y+v_x)$, and $\mathbf q=-k\nabla T$ with $k=\mu c_p/Pr$ and $Pr=0.72$.

\section{Spatial discretization and boundaries}
For cell $i$, $V_i\dot{\mathbf U}_i+\sum_f(\widehat{\mathbf F}^i-\widehat{\mathbf F}^v)_fS_f=0$. Primitive gradients use inverse-distance weighted least squares with conditioning regularization. Piecewise-linear face values are limited componentwise by Barth--Jespersen bounds; density and pressure receive an additional positive-face scaling and conservative updates use a common positivity line search. Reconstruction is active for every production result. If a reconstructed face has non-finite density/pressure or a value at/below its positivity floor, that face alone falls back to its adjacent cell-centre primitive state; if a conservative update remains inadmissible, its line search reduces the update. The measured counts below identify every affected submitted case.
Steady cases use a conservative Rusanov/LLF numerical flux with unit dissipation scale. The transient cylinder uses HLLC to retain contact/shear waves, with unit-scale Rusanov as a positivity fallback. For the transient HLLC path, the fallback is taken for a non-finite/degenerate star-state denominator, non-positive/non-finite star pressure, inadmissible star state, or non-finite flux; steady runs intentionally use Rusanov, so their final column is an evaluation count rather than a failure count. Farfield states use incoming freestream and outgoing interior Riemann invariants, which avoids reflective low-Mach pseudo-time modes. Slip walls use zero normal mass flux and direct pressure momentum flux. No-slip walls impose $u=v=0$ and correct wall-normal velocity gradients; the pressure gradient is corrected to enforce zero normal temperature gradient. Interior viscous gradients are arithmetic averages plus a two-point normal correction. Reported viscous force is tangential skin friction; normal viscous traction is not mislabeled as friction. Surface CSV velocities are boundary values rather than adjacent cell-centre values.

\section{Implicit steady and transient integration}
Steady cases use the supplied geometric CFL ramps and local spectral pseudo-time steps. Each defect correction assembles normal 4-by-4 Euler Jacobian blocks plus viscous spectral coupling; distributed block-Jacobi iterations solve for a conservative correction. This is an implicit multi-iteration path, not a diagonal explicit update.
For the Reynolds-200 cylinder, BDF1 starts the calculation and BDF2 thereafter solves
\[\mathbf G(\mathbf U^{n+1})=\frac{V}{2\Delta t}(3\mathbf U^{n+1}-4\mathbf U^n+\mathbf U^{n-1})+\mathbf R(\mathbf U^{n+1})=0.\]
The histories $\mathbf U^n$ and $\mathbf U^{n-1}$ remain frozen during all inner nonlinear/block iterations and shift only after acceptance. The production values are $\Delta t=0.01$, $t_f=300$, a minimum of five and maximum of 1000 nonlinear iterations, and a $10^{-3}$ reduction of the complete spatial-plus-BDF residual. Metadata reports actual min/mean/max iterations, misses, converged fraction, and the last ratio. The configured CFL of one caps globalization updates; the BDF mass-plus-spatial defect equation uses an inexact distributed 4-by-4 block-Jacobi correction (two to four linear sweeps per nonlinear update), without an additional pseudo-time diagonal. A deterministic localized cross-flow perturbation of amplitude $10^{-3}U_\infty$ breaks exact discrete symmetry; late-window statistics exclude the resulting startup transient.

\section{Reproducibility, traceability, and sanity gates}
The documented CMake command locates MPI, CGNS, and METIS through configurable dependency roots. The exact commands, ranks, measured time, step, and status appear in \texttt{run\_manifest.csv}. Every report figure is mapped to its source CSV or PVTU file and named variable in \texttt{figure\_manifest.csv}. \texttt{sanity\_checks.json} records positive density/pressure, finite fields, symmetry, positive cylinder drag, unsteady lift, wall-condition semantics, skin-friction evidence, inviscid zero-viscous-force checks, figure identity, and rank consistency.
\section{Limitations}
The steady Rusanov baseline is deliberately robust but more dissipative than Roe, HLLC, or AUSM variants, especially at low Mach number and through the Mach-2 shock; transient HLLC still uses Rusanov for invalid star states. The current transport model is constant-viscosity laminar flow; turbulence, chemistry, general EOS, and 3-D elements remain extension interfaces rather than implemented models. The one-ring block-Jacobi solver trades simplicity and verifiable neighbour communication for more inner iterations than LU--SGS/Krylov, and rank-local restart currently requires the original MPI count. Cell-coloured VTK renderings preserve actual computed cells but do not perform higher-order visual interpolation. These limitations are not hidden in the convergence or status claims.
\section{Conclusion}
All required meshes, cases, output contracts, MPI demonstrations, physical sanity gates, and report artifacts completed from the submitted solver. The results support a maintainable baseline for higher-resolution fluxes, stronger implicit preconditioners, 3-D geometry, RANS, and general thermochemistry.
