\title{Benchmark of a Distributed Cell-Centered Finite-Volume Solver\\
for Two-Dimensional Compressible Flow}
\author{Submitted solver team}
\date{10 August 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
A distributed-memory, cell-centered finite-volume solver was assessed on six
zero-incidence NACA0012 cases and two circular-cylinder cases. The method uses
weighted least-squares reconstruction, a Venkatakrishnan limiter with
Barth--Jespersen shock and positivity fallbacks, Rusanov fluxes, matrix-free
Newton--Krylov iteration, and a full-block Rusanov LU-SGS preconditioner. METIS
partitions the cell graph; each rank then retains owned cells, one-ring ghosts,
and neighbor schedules rather than a replicated mesh or state. All eight required
cases completed: the six NACA cases and the cylinder at $\Rey=20$ converged,
while the cylinder at $\Rey=200$ reached a statistically periodic state through
$t=300$. Over $t\in[200,300]$, the latter gave
$\overline C_D=1.1103912$, $C_{L,\mathrm{rms}}=0.1593857$, and $St=0.164678$
from the FFT bin ($0.162144$ from mean-upcrossing periods). All final fields
passed positivity, wall-condition, force-closure, and output consistency checks.
Principal limitations are Rusanov diffusion, no grid-refinement or external
reference-data error study, and timings from historical binaries rather than a
single controlled strong-scaling experiment.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
This benchmark tests one C++17/MPI solver on subsonic, transonic, and supersonic
NACA0012 flow and on steady and unsteady laminar cylinder flow. The report links
the method to residual, force, surface, and field data; separates final values
from post-transient statistics; and treats the machine-readable manifests as the
production record. The eight cases appear in \cref{tab:cases}. Seven steady runs
satisfy their original-baseline residual targets and full-order hold gates. The
eighth is a true BDF transient classified by force periodicity, not a steady
residual criterion.

\section{Governing Equations and Nondimensionalization}
\label{sec:equations}
The conservative state and two-dimensional equations are
\begin{equation}
 \vect U=[\rho,\rho u,\rho v,\rho E]^T,
 \qquad \frac{\partial\vect U}{\partial t}
 +\frac{\partial\vect F^i}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial\vect G^i}{\partial y}
 =\frac{\partial\vect F^v}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial\vect G^v}{\partial y},
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
\vect F^i=\begin{bmatrix}\rho u\\ \rho u^2+p\\ \rho uv\\u(\rho E+p)\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\vect G^i=\begin{bmatrix}\rho v\\ \rho uv\\ \rho v^2+p\\v(\rho E+p)\end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
The calorically perfect gas has $\gamma=1.4$, $R=1$, and $Pr=0.72$:
\begin{equation}
 p=(\gamma-1)\rho e,\quad E=e+\tfrac12(u^2+v^2),\quad
 T=\frac{p}{\rho R},\quad a=\sqrt{\gamma p/\rho}.
\end{equation}
For laminar cases, Newtonian stress and Fourier heat flux are
\begin{align}
 \tau_{xx}&=2\mu u_x-\tfrac23\mu(u_x+v_y),&
 \tau_{yy}&=2\mu v_y-\tfrac23\mu(u_x+v_y),\\
 \tau_{xy}&=\mu(u_y+v_x),&
 \vect q&=-k\nabla T,\qquad k=\mu c_p/Pr,
\end{align}
with $c_p=\gamma R/(\gamma-1)$. Constant viscosity obeys
$\mu=\rho_\infty U_\infty L_{\Rey}/\Rey$ and is zero in inviscid mode. The
viscous energy flux contains $u\tau_{xj}+v\tau_{yj}-q_j$ under this equation-sign
convention.

All cases use $\rho_\infty=U_\infty=L_{ref}=A_{ref}=1$. Pressure is scaled by
$\rho_\infty U_\infty^2$, time by $L_{ref}/U_\infty$, and
$p_\infty=1/(\gamma\Mach_\infty^2)$. With
$q_\infty=\tfrac12\rho_\infty U_\infty^2$,
\begin{equation}
 C_D=\frac{\vect F\cdot\vect e_D}{q_\infty A_{ref}},\quad
 C_L=\frac{\vect F\cdot\vect e_L}{q_\infty A_{ref}},\quad
 C_m=\frac{M_z}{q_\infty A_{ref}L_{ref}},\quad
 C_p=\frac{p-p_\infty}{q_\infty},\quad C_f=\frac{\tau_t}{q_\infty}.
\end{equation}

\section{Meshes, Boundary Tags, and Case Inputs}
\label{sec:meshes}
Rank 0 reads every CGNS base and zone. The reader accepts polygonal cells,
merges coincident inter-zone vertices, resolves each \code{BC_t} to its
\code{FamilyName_t}, and rejects a \code{FamilySpecified} boundary without a
family name. The NACA mesh has one discovered zone; the cylinder mesh has two.
A nominally 2-D mesh stored with physical dimension three is projected only when
its $z$ span is at most $10^{-7}$ of the in-plane diagonal.

Polygon areas and centroids use the shoelace formula. Edge midpoint and length
give the face center and measure; the unit normal points left-to-right or outward
at a boundary. Validation requires positive areas, complete incidences, and a
family tag on every boundary face. NACA0012 has 15,682 vertices, 20,816 cells,
36,498 faces, 80 \code{bc-2} faces, and 404 \code{bc-4} faces. CylinderB1 has
9,995 vertices, 10,185 cells, 20,180 faces, 20 \code{FAR} faces, and 100
\code{WALL} faces.

\section{Spatial Discretization}
\label{sec:spatial}
For cell $i$ of area $V_i$, the cell-centered residual is
\begin{equation}
 \vect R_i=\sum_{f\in\partial i}s_{if}L_f
 [\widehat{\vect F}^{i}_n(\vect U_L,\vect U_R)-\widehat{\vect F}^{v}_n],
 \qquad s_{if}=+1\ (i=L),\quad -1\ (i=R).
\end{equation}
Interior flux is added to the left cell and subtracted from the right. Boundary
faces replace the exterior state or flux as described in \cref{sec:bc}.

Inverse-distance-squared weighted least squares reconstructs
$\vect W=[\rho,u,v,p]^T$. Neighbor and boundary ghost samples form one
$2\times2$ normal matrix for all components. Corrected velocity and temperature
face gradients use the mean cell gradient plus a normal correction that recovers
the cell-to-cell difference. With allowed extremum distance $a$, unlimited
increment $b$, and scale regularization $\epsilon^2$, the limiter is
\begin{equation}
 \phi_V=\frac{a^2+2a|b|+\epsilon^2}{a^2+a|b|+2b^2+\epsilon^2},\qquad
 \epsilon^2=q_{scale}^2(5h/L_{ref})^3.
\end{equation}
A density or pressure neighborhood ratio approaching 1.75 blends toward the
stricter Barth--Jespersen bound; at 1.75 it marks a shock-fallback cell. An
inadmissible face retries that bound, scales the increment for positive density
and pressure, and only then uses the cell state. Update line searches also
require admissibility. No final case used positivity scaling or a first-order
face fallback. Final Mach-2 NACA states had 197 inviscid and 141 laminar
shock-fallback cells; all others had zero.

The Rusanov flux is
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{\vect F}^{i}_n=\tfrac12(\vect F_n(\vect U_L)+\vect F_n(\vect U_R))
 -\tfrac12s_{max}(\vect U_R-\vect U_L),\quad
 s_{max}=\max(|u_{n,L}|+a_L,|u_{n,R}|+a_R).
\end{equation}
Its dissipation scale is one, and it needs no entropy fix. Viscous fluxes use
reconstructed $u,v,T$ gradients and the constitutive laws above. At a no-slip
wall, normal velocity gradients enforce the stationary wall and the normal
temperature gradient is removed. Pressure contributes $pL_f\vect n$ to body
force. Total viscous force uses complete traction, while $C_f$ uses only signed
tangential traction; the pressure and viscous force columns therefore remain
separate.

\section{Boundary Conditions}
\label{sec:bc}
Farfield faces use a characteristic exterior state. Supersonic inflow takes
freestream and supersonic outflow takes interior state. At subsonic faces,
incoming and outgoing acoustic invariants are combined; entropy and tangential
velocity come from interior on outflow and freestream on inflow. This state
enters the Rusanov flux.

Solid walls use direct pressure flux $[0,pn_x,pn_y,0]^T$. A slip ghost mirrors
normal velocity; surface output removes only that component and preserves
tangential velocity. A no-slip ghost mirrors both components; its viscous flux
sets wall velocity to zero and normal heat flux to zero. Surface CSV rows are
boundary values, not adjacent centers. No-slip $u=v=0$ exactly, the largest
inviscid normal speed is $1.11\times10^{-16}$, and inviscid viscous forces are
zero.

\section{Implicit and Transient Time Integration}
\label{sec:time}
\subsection{Steady solve and accepted-order continuation}
The primary steady update is matrix-free Newton with restarted,
right-preconditioned GMRES. Its preconditioner freezes a first-order full
$4\times4$ conservative Rusanov Jacobian. LU-SGS orders by global cell ID, uses
at least three forward/backward sweeps, block-lags remote corrections, and
exchanges them between half-sweeps. The diagonal includes local pseudo time,
with
\begin{equation}
 \Delta\tau_i=\frac{\mathrm{CFL}V_i}
 {\sum_fL_f[(|u_n|+a)+2\nu_{eff}/d_f]},\qquad
 \nu_{eff}=\frac{\mu}{\rho}\max(4/3,\gamma/Pr),
\end{equation}
and no viscous term for $\mu=0$.

Inviscid reconstruction starts at blend zero, is held for
$N_h=\min(500,\max(1,N_{ramp}/4))$ accepted updates, and ramps smoothly to one
over another $N_h$ accepted updates. Viscous gradients remain active. Completion
requires blend one,
$\|R\|_2\le\|R_{0,\mathrm{original}}\|_2 10^{-r_{target}}$, and at least
$\max(50,\min(250,N_{ramp}/10))$ accepted full-order updates, plus positivity
and physics gates. Rejected attempts do not advance holds. The original global
MPI residual remains the convergence/reporting baseline across continuation and
restart. The residual on entering full order is diagnostic only.

For steady runs, \code{step} is the external nonlinear-attempt counter and
therefore includes rejected attempts. Accepted-step counters used by the
continuation and hold gates are separate.

Every ordinary attempt uses JFNK/LU-SGS. After failed globalization, bounded
decade-CFL retries and endpoint rescue require a strictly lower actual residual.
After three attempts without a meaningful best decrease at fixed blend, an
implicit bridge may solve $(\sigma/\mathrm{CFL}I+J_{frozen})\delta=-R$. It uses
the largest globally positivity-safe scale and permits nonmonotone steps only
below 1.25 times cycle-entry residual. CFL starts at 0.1, halves after more than
5 percent growth, and increases 20 percent after a decrease. Each cycle is
capped at 500 accepted steps and must improve the best within 500; a bounded new
cycle may begin at the CFL floor. A separate strict-decrease LU-SGS fallback is
limited to 64 consecutive steps. These are secondary robustness paths, not
separate production results.

\subsection{True BDF transient for cylinder Re200}
The transient has an outer physical-time loop and inner nonlinear loop. Step one
uses true BDF1; subsequent steps use
\begin{equation}
 \vect G(\vect U^{n+1})=\frac{V}{\Delta t}
 (\tfrac32\vect U^{n+1}-2\vect U^n+\tfrac12\vect U^{n-1})
 +\vect R(\vect U^{n+1})=0.
\end{equation}
BDF1 has coefficient one and BDF2 adds $3V/(2\Delta t)$ to the physical
diagonal. During every inner solve, $U^n$ and $U^{n-1}$ remain frozen; histories
advance only after acceptance. Matrix-free GMRES and full-block Rusanov LU-SGS
act on the total spatial-plus-physical residual.

Production used exactly $\Delta t=0.01$, $t_f=300$, fixed pseudo-CFL one, 5--1000
inner iterations, and a $10^{-3}$ target. All 30,000 steps used exactly five
iterations: observed min/mean/max are 5/5/5, misses zero, target-met fraction
1.0, and last ratio $8.3834023\times10^{-5}$.
\sourcefile{results/cylinder_m010_laminar_re200/stdout.log} contains 30,000
unique step records; 197 resume-overlap duplicates are value-identical and were
collapsed by step. The resulting inner-ratio minimum/median/linearly
interpolated 95th-percentile/maximum is
$2.11219\times10^{-5}$/$1.17154\times10^{-4}$/$4.88800\times10^{-4}$/$9.99889\times10^{-4}$.
Thus every physical step met $10^{-3}$ at exactly five nonlinear iterations.

\section{MPI Parallelization and METIS Partitioning}
\label{sec:mpi}
Rank 0 forms an undirected cell graph with one edge per interior face and calls
METIS k-way partitioning. Rank-local meshes contain owned cells first, one-ring
ghosts next, and only faces needed by owned residuals. Schedules list exact
neighbor send/receive IDs. Nonblocking \code{MPI_Isend}/\code{MPI_Irecv}
exchanges state, gradients, and limiter data; MPI reductions form residuals,
forces, and statistics. Solver iterations replicate neither the full mesh nor
the full conservative state. Startup mesh distribution and restart/final-output
gathers are outside the iteration path.

\section{Output, Reproducibility, and Sanity Checks}
\label{sec:repro}
Each canonical directory contains metadata/status JSON, partition, residual,
force and surface CSV, actual-cell VTU, restart files, and stdout. Final force,
surface, field, and status steps match. History CSVs contain samples at the
configured output cadence plus an unconditional final-state row; they are not
necessarily one row per nonlinear attempt. Their final rows always match the
final field and surface state. All canonical runs used eight ranks.
Exact commands, hashes, timestamps, restart segments, and accounting are in
\sourcefile{report/run_manifest.csv}. Re20 wall time is the final resumed segment
because cumulative active time was not logged. Re200 separately records 20,988.5
s cumulative active time across five segments.

The standard-library postprocessor reads only canonical CSV/VTU files, writes
deterministic SVG and derived data, and hashes every source and output. Check mode
verifies without rewriting. \sourcefile{report/sanity_checks.json} reports an
overall pass for positive density/pressure, nontrivial fields and $C_p$, NACA
symmetry, positive cylinder drag, wall semantics, force closure, final-step
consistency, Re200 periodicity, and separate Mach/pressure mappings. These are
internal checks, not a grid-convergence or experimental-validation study.

\section{Limitations and Failure Analysis}
\label{sec:limitations}
No canonical case failed; rejected implicit attempts and diagnostics are not
presented as production flow results.
\begin{itemize}
\item Rusanov diffusion can broaden shocks, layers, and vortices.
\item One mesh per geometry establishes no mesh-independent accuracy, and no
experimental or high-order reference error norm was computed.
\item Order continuation and bounded retries complicate nonlinear-path
interpretation. Final outputs are full order, but original-baseline reduction
includes continuation; full-order-only reduction is diagnostic.
\item The Mach-2 laminar NACA case is the weakest steady claim: it stopped at
the exact 500+500+250 accepted-phase minimum, achieved only 1.201935
full-order-only orders, and retained late drag drift. More full-order iterations
and mesh refinement are needed despite its valid \code{converged} gate status.
\item Re5000 wall resolution lacks $y^+$, profile, or refinement evidence. The
large Mach-2 viscous drag is the submitted-grid result, not a validated reference.
\item Re200 statistics contain about 16 crossing cycles. Longer records and more
meshes would better quantify spectral uncertainty. FFT-bin and crossing estimates
are reported separately rather than averaged.
\item Parallel timings mix executable hashes and host load. Same-binary repeated
$np=1,2,4,8$ runs are required for rigorous scaling.
\item Percentile clipping improves visibility but hides extrema in the color
mapping; raw extrema remain in \sourcefile{report/results_summary.json}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusions}
All eight cases completed. Seven steady cases met original-baseline residual,
full-order hold, positivity, and physics gates. The true BDF1/BDF2 Re200 run
completed 30,000 steps with exactly five converged inner iterations per step and
gave a stationary vortex street with $\overline C_D=1.1103912$ and
$St\simeq0.162$--0.165, depending on the stated estimator. Surface semantics,
force splits, final-step consistency, and Mach/pressure mapping passed. Rank
comparisons agree closely, subject to the executable-hash caveat. Priorities are
mesh refinement, a less dissipative shock-capable flux, and controlled scaling.
