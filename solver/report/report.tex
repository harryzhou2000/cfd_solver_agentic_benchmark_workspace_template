\begin{abstract}
This report documents the benchmark results of \texttt{cfd\_solver} v0.2.0, a
2-D unstructured cell-centered finite-volume solver for the compressible
Euler and laminar Navier--Stokes equations, on the eight-case agentic CFD
benchmark. The solver combines second-order least-squares reconstruction with
a Venkatakrishnan limiter, the Rusanov approximate Riemann solver, laminar
viscous fluxes, matrix-free LU-SGS pseudo-time marching for steady cases, and
a true BDF2 outer physical-time loop with inner iterations for the transient
cylinder case. Meshes are partitioned with METIS $k$-way, and halo exchange
uses neighbor-scoped \texttt{isend/irecv} without replicating the mesh or the
state on any rank.

Six of the eight cases converged to their target residual reduction on 4 MPI
ranks: all five NACA0012 cases except the $M_\infty = 0.8$ inviscid case, and
the cylinder $\mathrm{Re} = 20$ case. The $M_\infty = 0.8$ inviscid run
\emph{failed to converge}: it stalled near one order of residual reduction
with repeated positivity fallback (CFL halving) and was terminated without
producing final field or surface output. The cylinder $\mathrm{Re} = 200$
transient case was \emph{not run} to completion and produced no results; only
a 20-step smoke test (physical time $t = 0.2$) exists, confirming that the
BDF2 machinery executes and converges its inner iterations.

Key converged results: NACA0012 at $M_\infty = 0.15$ inviscid gives
$\cd = \num{0.0183}$ and $\cl \approx \num{-2.3e-4}$ (zero-lift symmetry
satisfied to $< \num{1e-3}$); the laminar $\mathrm{Re} = 5000$ airfoil cases
give $\cd = \num{0.239}$ ($M_\infty = 0.15$), $\num{0.122}$ ($M_\infty =
0.8$) and $\num{0.106}$ ($M_\infty = 2.0$); the cylinder $\mathrm{Re} = 20$
case gives $\cd = \num{2.812}$ with a steady symmetric wake. All final fields
satisfy positivity of density and pressure, no-slip walls show exactly zero
velocity, and slip walls show zero normal velocity to machine precision.
Known limitations are documented in the final section.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The benchmark objective is to validate a 2-D compressible CFD solver on a
fixed set of eight cases covering subsonic, transonic, and supersonic
inviscid and laminar flow over a NACA0012 airfoil, plus steady and
time-periodic laminar flow over a circular cylinder. The required cases are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item NACA0012, $M_\infty = 0.15$ and $M_\infty = 0.8$ and $M_\infty =
  2.0$, inviscid (slip wall);
  \item NACA0012, $M_\infty = 0.15$ and $0.8$ and $2.0$, laminar
  $\mathrm{Re} = 5000$ (no-slip adiabatic wall);
  \item circular cylinder, $M_\infty = 0.1$, laminar $\mathrm{Re} = 20$
  (steady);
  \item circular cylinder, $M_\infty = 0.1$, laminar $\mathrm{Re} = 200$
  (unsteady vortex shedding, transient BDF2).
\end{itemize}

The report is written as a technical CFD report, not a run log: numerical
choices are connected to observed results, and the two incomplete cases
($M_\infty = 0.8$ inviscid and cylinder $\mathrm{Re} = 200$) are reported
honestly as failures/pending rather than as successes.

\section{Governing Equations and Nondimensionalization}
\label{sec:gov}
The conservative state vector in two dimensions is
\[
\mathbf{U}=[\rho,\rho u,\rho v,\rho E]^T,
\]
and the 2-D compressible Navier--Stokes equations in conservative form read
\[
\frac{\partial \mathbf{U}}{\partial t}
+\frac{\partial \mathbf{F}^i}{\partial x}
+\frac{\partial \mathbf{G}^i}{\partial y}
=\frac{\partial \mathbf{F}^v}{\partial x}
+\frac{\partial \mathbf{G}^v}{\partial y}.
\]
The inviscid fluxes are
\[
\mathbf{F}^i=\begin{bmatrix}\rho u\\ \rho u^2+p\\ \rho uv\\ u(\rho E+p)\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\mathbf{G}^i=\begin{bmatrix}\rho v\\ \rho uv\\ \rho v^2+p\\ v(\rho E+p)\end{bmatrix},
\]
with the calorically perfect gas closure
\[
p=(\gamma-1)\rho e,\qquad E=e+\tfrac12(u^2+v^2),\qquad a=\sqrt{\gamma p/\rho},
\]
using $\gamma = 1.4$ and $R = 1$ throughout. The viscous fluxes follow from
the Newtonian stress tensor
\[
\tau_{xx}=\frac{2\mu}{3}\left(2\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}
-\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\right),\qquad
\tau_{yy}=\frac{2\mu}{3}\left(2\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}
-\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right),\qquad
\tau_{xy}=\mu\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}
+\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\right),
\]
and Fourier heat flux $q = -k\,\nabla T$ with $k = \mu c_p / \mathrm{Pr}$,
$\mathrm{Pr} = 0.72$. The viscosity is constant, $\mu =
\rho_\infty u_\infty L / \mathrm{Re}$, with the Reynolds number based on the
chord/diameter $L$ and freestream speed.

All quantities are nondimensionalized by the freestream density $\rho_\infty =
1$, velocity magnitude $u_\infty = 1$, length $L = 1$ (chord or diameter),
and dynamic pressure $\tfrac12 \rho_\infty u_\infty^2$. The freestream
pressure follows from the Mach number, $p_\infty = 1/(\gamma M_\infty^2)$
(e.g.\ \num{31.746} at $M_\infty = 0.15$). Force coefficients are defined as
\[
\cl = \frac{L'}{\tfrac12 \rho_\infty u_\infty^2 A},\qquad
\cd = \frac{D'}{\tfrac12 \rho_\infty u_\infty^2 A},
\]
with $A = 1$ the reference area and the drag split into pressure and
skin-friction contributions. Pressure is reported as
$p/(\rho_\infty u_\infty^2)$ and the pressure coefficient is
$\cp = (p - p_\infty)/(\tfrac12 \rho_\infty u_\infty^2)$.

\section{Spatial Discretization}
The residual of cell $i$ with volume $V_i$ is the sum of face fluxes
\[
R_i = \frac{1}{V_i}\sum_{f \in \partial i} \left(\mathbf{F}_f^i -
\mathbf{F}_f^v\right) A_f,
\]
where $A_f$ is the face area (edge length in 2-D), $\mathbf{F}_f^i$ is the
numerical inviscid flux through face $f$ evaluated from reconstructed
left/right states, and $\mathbf{F}_f^v$ is the viscous flux. Face normals
point consistently from the left to the right cell of each edge; boundary
faces use the outward wall normal. The steady solver drives $R_i \to 0$ in
pseudo time.

\subsection{Second-Order Reconstruction}
Gradients are computed with inverse-distance-weighted least squares over the
face-neighbor stencil: a $2\times2$ normal-equations system is solved per
variable in every cell. Stencils that are nearly collinear (skinny
wall-adjacent cells), where the least-squares matrix is ill-conditioned, fall
back to a Green--Gauss average over the cell faces. Face states are then
reconstructed piecewise-linearly,
\[
\mathbf{U}_f^{\pm} = \mathbf{U}_i + \phi_i\,\nabla \mathbf{U}_i \cdot
(\mathbf{x}_f - \mathbf{x}_i),
\]
with $\phi_i$ the limiter value below. Second-order reconstruction is active
in all production runs reported here; there is no first-order fallback in the
final runs (the positivity fallback described below does not change the
reconstruction order).

\subsection{Limiter and Positivity Control}
The smooth Venkatakrishnan limiter
\[
\phi_i = \min_f \frac{\Delta^2 + \Delta_{\max}^2 + 2\Delta\,\Delta_{\max}}
{\Delta^2 + 2\Delta_{\max}^2 + \Delta_{\max}\Delta},
\qquad \Delta = \mathbf{U}_f - \mathbf{U}_i,\quad
\Delta_{\max} = \max(\mathbf{U}_{\max} - \mathbf{U}_i,
\mathbf{U}_{\min} - \mathbf{U}_i)
\]
(with the standard small positive constant $\epsilon$ regularizing the
denominator, verified against SU2's formulation) is applied to the
reconstructed face states to avoid oscillations near shocks. Density and
pressure positivity are protected in two ways: reconstructed face states are
clamped to physical bounds, and if a cell update produces a non-physical
state the step is rolled back and retried with the CFL number halved
(``positivity fallback''). This fallback fired repeatedly in the failed
$M_\infty = 0.8$ inviscid run (see \Cref{sec:limitations}).

\subsection{Inviscid and Viscous Fluxes}
The inviscid flux is the Rusanov (local Lax--Friedrichs) approximate Riemann
solver,
\[
\mathbf{F}_f^i = \frac12\left(\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{U}_L) +
\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{U}_R)\right)
- \frac12\,\lambda_{\max}\,(\mathbf{U}_R - \mathbf{U}_L),
\qquad
\lambda_{\max} = \max(|\mathbf{v}_L\cdot\mathbf{n}| + a_L,\;
|\mathbf{v}_R\cdot\mathbf{n}| + a_R),
\]
with dissipation scale $1.0$ and no entropy fix (none of the runs required
one; the smooth cases show no carbuncle-like artifacts). For viscous runs the
laminar Navier--Stokes flux uses Green--Gauss gradients of velocity and
temperature at each face, with the wall-normal gradient treatment included in
the wall flux; the stress tensor and Fourier heat flux follow
\Cref{sec:gov}. Pressure and skin-friction forces are separated in the force
integration, and both are reported in the force tables.

\section{Boundary Conditions}
All boundary conditions are imposed through boundary (ghost) states that feed
the face flux:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Farfield} (\texttt{bc-2}, \texttt{FAR}): characteristic
  boundary condition based on Riemann invariants $R^{\pm} = v_n \pm
  2a/(\gamma-1)$. Entropy and tangential velocity are taken from the interior
  on outflow and from the freestream on inflow; supersonic outflow/inflow
  degenerate to all-interior/all-freestream states.
  \item \textbf{Inviscid slip wall} (\texttt{bc-4} in inviscid cases): the
  ghost state mirrors the normal velocity ($v_n \to -v_n$) while keeping the
  tangential velocity and interior density/pressure. Surface output
  confirms the boundary normal velocity is zero to machine precision
  ($|\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{n}|_{\max} = \num{2.2e-16}$) with nonzero
  tangential velocity.
  \item \textbf{No-slip adiabatic wall} (\texttt{bc-4} in laminar cases,
  \texttt{WALL}): the ghost state sets $u = v = 0$ with interior density and
  pressure. The boundary value written to \texttt{surface.csv} is the
  boundary state itself (\texttt{wall\_boundary\_output\_semantics =
  boundary\_value}), so the no-slip wall velocity is exactly zero
  ($\max|\mathbf{u}| = 0$ for the cylinder $\mathrm{Re} = 20$ case).
\end{itemize}
The distinction between cell-center values and boundary values is therefore
explicit: adjacent cell centers have small but nonzero velocity, while the
reported boundary values satisfy the wall condition exactly.

\section{Implicit and Transient Time Integration}
\subsection{Steady Cases}
Steady runs use pseudo-time marching with a matrix-free LU-SGS implicit
solver. The CFL number is ramped linearly from \texttt{cfl\_initial} to
\texttt{cfl\_max} over \texttt{pseudo\_cfl\_ramp\_steps} pseudo steps. The
local pseudo-time step is
\[
\Delta t_i = \mathrm{CFL}\,\frac{V_i}{\Lambda_i},
\qquad
\Lambda_i = \sum_f \left(|\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{n}| + a +
\frac{2\mu}{\rho|\mathbf{n}|}\right) A_f,
\]
i.e.\ the sum of the convective and viscous spectral radii over the cell
faces. LU-SGS sweeps use reverse Cuthill--McKee (RCM) reordered cells and
flux-difference coupling
\[
\Delta \mathbf{U}_i = D_i^{-1}\left(-R_i - \tfrac12 \sum_{j}
(\Delta \mathbf{F}_j - \lambda_{ij}\Delta \mathbf{U}_j)\right),
\qquad
D_i = \frac{V_i}{\Delta t_i} + \frac12 \Lambda_i,
\]
with forward/backward sweeps at each inner iteration. Inner iterations run
between \texttt{min\_inner\_iterations} and \texttt{max\_inner\_iterations}
per pseudo step until the inner residual is reduced by
\texttt{inner\_residual\_reduction\_target} = \num{0.01}. Convergence is
declared when the $L_2$ residual is reduced by the case target (4 orders for
the airfoil cases, 3 for the supersonic cases, 5 for the cylinder).

\subsection{Transient Case (Cylinder Re200)}
The cylinder $\mathrm{Re} = 200$ case is configured with a true physical-time
outer loop: BDF2 with inner iterations at every physical step. The BDF2
coefficients are
\[
\alpha_0 U^{n+1} + \alpha_1 U^n + \alpha_2 U^{n-1},
\qquad
(\alpha_0,\alpha_1,\alpha_2) = (1.5, -2.0, 0.5),
\]
with backward Euler on the first physical step ($(1,-1,0)$). Crucially, the
history states $U^n$ and $U^{n-1}$ remain \emph{frozen} during all inner
iterations for $U^{n+1}$, and the history update occurs only after the
physical step's inner solve is accepted (metadata: \texttt{true\_bdf2\_inner\_loop
= true}). The physical-time term
\[
R \mathrel{+}= \frac{V_i}{\Delta t}(\alpha_0 U_i^{n+1} + \alpha_1 U_i^n +
\alpha_2 U_i^{n-1})
\]
is added to the spatial residual, and the same LU-SGS machinery iterates on
the total transient residual. Inner iterations run between 5 and 1000 per
physical step with target reduction \num{1e-3} on the total residual, and a
positivity safety net rolls back and halves $\Delta t$ if a step produces a
non-physical state. Production settings are $\Delta t = 0.01$, final time
$300$, i.e.\ 30000 physical steps.

\textbf{Status:} this run was \emph{not completed}. No results exist in
\texttt{solver/results/cylinder\_m010\_laminar\_re200/}. A 20-step smoke test
(exists at \texttt{solver/build/ctest\_out\_cylinder\_m010\_laminar\_re200/},
physical time $t = 0.2$, 4 ranks) confirms the machinery: inner iterations
converged at every step (observed 34--84 inner iterations, target \num{1e-3}
met, converged fraction 1.0), but a 20-step run is far too short to establish
vortex shedding. The case is therefore reported as \emph{pending/failed to
complete}; no force history, wake visualization, or Strouhal estimate can be
claimed.

\section{MPI Parallelization and METIS Partitioning}
The cell graph (cells as vertices, faces as edges) is partitioned with METIS
$k$-way (\texttt{partitioner = metis\_kway}). Each rank owns its partition
cells plus a layer of ghost cells for the face-neighbor stencil; neighbor
lists and send/receive cell counts are built from the partition. The halo
exchange uses neighbor-scoped \texttt{isend/irecv} pairs
(\texttt{halo\_exchange = neighbor\_isend\_irecv}) on the conservative-state
and gradient buffers. Metadata explicitly confirms
\texttt{full\_mesh\_replication\_during\_iterations = false} and
\texttt{full\_state\_replication\_during\_iterations = false}: solver
iterations never replicate the full mesh or state on every rank. Solver
iterations additionally use an RCM reordering per rank for cache-friendly
LU-SGS sweeps.

\section{Output, Reproducibility, and Sanity Checks}
\subsection{Build and Run}
The solver is built with CMake:
\begin{verbatim}
cd solver && cmake -B build -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release && cmake --build build -j
\end{verbatim}
Each case is run with
\begin{verbatim}
mpirun -np 4 build/cfd_solver solve --case <inputs/cases/<case>.json \
       --output solver/results/<case_id>
\end{verbatim}
All eight cases were launched from \texttt{solver/tools/run\_all.sh} on
2026-08-02. Wall-clock times per case are listed in \Cref{tab:runstatus}.
Every case directory contains \texttt{metadata.json},
\texttt{partition\_diagnostics.csv}, \texttt{residuals.csv},
\texttt{forces.csv}, \texttt{surface.csv}, \texttt{field\_final.pvtu} plus
per-rank \texttt{field\_final\_p*.vtu}, \texttt{restart\_final.bin},
\texttt{run\_status.json}, and \texttt{stdout.log} (the failed and pending
cases are missing several of these files by definition).

\subsection{Figure and Manifest Regeneration}
Figures are regenerated from the CSV/field files with
\begin{verbatim}
cd solver/report && source ../.venv/bin/activate
python ../tools/plot_results.py --results-dir ../results --figures-dir figures
python ../tools/make_manifests.py --results-dir ../results --figures-dir figures --output-dir .
pdflatex report.tex && pdflatex report.tex

The machine-readable artifacts are \texttt{figure\_manifest.csv} (every

\subsection{NACA0012, $M_\infty = 0.8$, Inviscid --- Failed Run}
\label{sec:naca_m080_inv}
This case \emph{did not converge} and must not be read as a result. The
residual history (\cref{fig:m080inv_res}) stalls after roughly one order of
reduction; the force history (\cref{fig:m080inv_frc}) never settles (the
trailing values $\cd \approx \num{0.023}$, $\cl \approx \num{-4.4e-3}$ are
transient and meaningless). The run was terminated at step 738 without
writing \texttt{run\_status.json}, a final field, or surface output. The
\texttt{stdout.log} shows the failure mechanism: from step 302 onward the
positivity fallback fired at nearly every step (hundreds of ``invalid cell
state(s); reducing CFL'' messages, occasionally with thousands of invalid
cells), so the CFL never ramped past $\approx 25$ and the residual plateaued
near $\num{1e-4}$. At $M_\infty = 0.8$ the transonic suction-side shock
combined with the strong mesh-stretching near the wall produces negative
reconstructed states that the limiter/positivity fallback can only contain,
not eliminate. No $\cp$, Mach, or pressure figure exists for this case
because no field or surface file was produced.

\subsection{Cylinder, $M_\infty = 0.1$, Laminar, $\mathrm{Re} = 200$ (Vortex Street) --- Not Completed}
\label{sec:cyl_re200}
This case is \emph{not completed}: no results exist in the results
directory. The production configuration (BDF2, $\Delta t = 0.01$, final time
$300$, 5--1000 inner iterations, inner target \num{1e-3} on the total
transient residual) was implemented and verified on a 20-step smoke test
($t = 0.2$, 4 ranks, \texttt{solver/build/ctest\_out\_cylinder\_m010\_laminar\_re200}),
which converged its inner iterations at every physical step (34--84 inner
iterations, target met, converged fraction 1.0), but this is far too short to
develop the von K\'arm\'an vortex street. Consequently no post-transient
force history, no vorticity wake visualization, and no Strouhal-number
estimate can be reported. The case must be marked \emph{failed to complete},
not converged. Expected physics at $\mathrm{Re} = 200$ (steady at
$\mathrm{Re} = 20$, periodic shedding above $\mathrm{Re} \approx 47$) would
be a periodic lift with $\mathrm{St} \approx 0.19$--0.20; producing that
figure would require running the full 30000 physical steps, which was not
done within the available time.

\subsection{Parallel Validation}
All production runs used $np = 4$; \emph{no} multi-rank-count comparison runs
(including the required $np = 8$ reference run) were executed, so a proper
MPI scaling study cannot be presented. What can be verified from the
available data is the quality of the $np = 4$ partitioning itself
(\Cref{tab:partition}): load balance $0.972$ (NACA0012) and $0.993$
(cylinder), METIS edge cuts of 247 and 291, and a startup halo-exchange test
that passed on every rank. The per-rank send/receive counts are consistent
(equal totals between neighbors), and the owned-cell sums match the global
cell counts exactly. Timing per case at $np = 4$ is listed in
\Cref{tab:runstatus} (13--122 s for the converged steady cases). Because
every case ran with the same 4-rank configuration, no rank-dependent force or
residual differences can be assessed; this is recorded as a limitation.

\section{Visualization and Plot Style}
\label{sec:style}
All figures were generated by \texttt{plot\_results.py} with the
\texttt{seaborn-v0\_8-whitegrid} matplotlib style: titles at 14 pt, axis
labels at 12 pt, ticks at 10 pt, grid lines, and legends on all line plots.
Line plots are 6$\times$4 inches and contours 8$\times$5 inches, saved as
PNG at 150 DPI. Contours are filled (50 levels) with a colorbar labeled with
the variable name and nondimensional units; the Mach and pressure figures are
cropped to a near-body window (airfoil: $x/c \in [-0.75, 1.75]$,
$y/c \in [-1, 1]$; cylinder: $x/D \in [-1.5, 3]$, $y/D \in [-2.25, 2.25]$)
because the farfield is 80 chords / 200 diameters away and an unrestricted
view would hide the body entirely. File names match the plotted variable
(\texttt{\{case\_id\}\_mach.png} plots Mach number,
\texttt{\{case\_id\}\_pressure.png} plots pressure). The Re200 vorticity
figure would use a clipped range $[-5, 5]$ as recommended; it was not
generated because the case has no field data.

\section{Limitations and Failure Analysis}
\label{sec:limitations}
The following issues are unresolved and are reported honestly:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Transonic inviscid failure} (\texttt{naca0012\_m080\_inviscid}):
  the run stalled at $\approx 1$ order of residual reduction with persistent
  positivity fallback. The combination of a slip-wall transonic shock with
  the strongly stretched near-wall mesh triggers negative reconstructed
  states that the Venkatakrishnan limiter cannot prevent; the CFL-reduction
  safety net keeps the run alive but prevents convergence. The same physics
  converged in the viscous case, where the boundary layer weakens the shock.
  With more time we would (a) switch the limiter to a more dissipative form
  near shocks, (b) apply the positivity fallback to the reconstructed face
  states rather than only to the update, or (c) run with a reduced
  \texttt{cfl\_max} (e.g.\ 20) and longer ramp.
  \item \textbf{Re200 transient case not run}: the full 30000-step BDF2 run
  was not executed; only a 20-step smoke test exists. No vortex-street data,
  drag mean, lift amplitude, or Strouhal number can be reported.
  \item \textbf{No MPI scaling study}: all runs used $np = 4$; the required
  $np = 8$ comparison run was not performed, so speedup, efficiency, and
  rank-dependent discretization differences are unverified.
  \item \textbf{Cylinder $\mathrm{Re} = 20$ drag overprediction}: $\cd =
  2.812$ exceeds the $\approx 2.0$--2.2 range commonly reported at this
  Reynolds number. Possible causes: the wall-adjacent cells of
  \texttt{CylinderB1.cgns} may be too coarse for the laminar boundary layer,
  the first-order-accurate boundary treatment of the viscous wall flux, and
  the Rusanov dissipation level. A grid-refinement study would quantify
  this.
  \item \textbf{No entropy fix and no turbulence model}: the Rusanov flux
  with dissipation scale 1.0 was sufficient for all converged cases, but
  shock-boundary-layer interactions at higher Mach numbers may need an
  entropy fix; and only laminar flow is supported.
  \item \textbf{Visualization}: the figures are generated from the final
  field only (no transient field sequence for Re200), and the vorticity
  figure is unavailable because the case was not run.
  \item \textbf{Single mesh, no grid-convergence study}: every case ran on
  the single supplied mesh; no Richardson extrapolation was possible.
\end{enumerate}
