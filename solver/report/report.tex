\begin{abstract}
This report describes \texttt{cfd-fv2d}, an original C++17/MPI cell-centered
finite-volume solver for the two-dimensional compressible Navier--Stokes
equations of a calorically perfect gas, written for the
\texttt{cfd\_solver\_agentic\_benchmark}. The solver reads the supplied CGNS
meshes, partitions the cell adjacency graph with METIS, integrates the
conservative equations with a second-order reconstructed Roe/Rusanov
discretization and a Venkatakrishnan limiter, and advances the implicit
dual-time system with a distributed symmetric Gauss--Seidel relaxation
(a matrix-free preconditioned GMRES variant is also implemented). All eight
benchmark cases are reported: six NACA0012 configurations and two laminar
cylinder configurations, including the Reynolds 200 vortex-street transient.
Convergence status, force coefficients, MPI rank-count comparisons,
visualizations, and known limitations are summarized below and traceable to
the submitted CSV, field, and JSON files.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The benchmark requires an end-to-end unstructured CFD solver: mesh import,
METIS partitioning, MPI halo communication, a conservative second-order
finite-volume residual, an implicit nonlinear solver, a true BDF2 transient
loop for the cylinder Reynolds 200 case, complete machine-readable outputs,
visualizations, and an academic-style report. This document connects the
numerical choices in the source to the measured residuals, forces, fields,
and parallel behavior for the eight required cases
(Table~\ref{tab:cases}).

\section{Governing Equations and Nondimensionalization}
The conservative state is
\[
\mathbf{U}=[\rho,\rho u,\rho v,\rho E]^T,
\]
and the 2-D compressible Navier--Stokes equations are written as
\[
\frac{\partial \mathbf{U}}{\partial t}
+\frac{\partial \mathbf{F}^i}{\partial x}
+\frac{\partial \mathbf{G}^i}{\partial y}
=\frac{\partial \mathbf{F}^v}{\partial x}
+\frac{\partial \mathbf{G}^v}{\partial y}.
\]
The inviscid fluxes are
\[
\mathbf{F}^i=\begin{bmatrix}\rho u\\ \rho u^2+p\\ \rho uv\\ u(\rho E+p)\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\mathbf{G}^i=\begin{bmatrix}\rho v\\ \rho uv\\ \rho v^2+p\\ v(\rho E+p)\end{bmatrix},
\]
with the calorically perfect gas closure
\[
p=(\gamma-1)\rho e,\qquad E=e+\tfrac12(u^2+v^2),\qquad a=\sqrt{\gamma p/\rho}.
\]
For viscous cases the Newtonian stress tensor is
\[
\tau_{xx}=2\mu u_x-\frac{2}{3}\mu(u_x+v_y),\quad
\tau_{yy}=2\mu v_y-\frac{2}{3}\mu(u_x+v_y),\quad
\tau_{xy}=\mu(u_y+v_x),
\]
and the Fourier heat flux is $\mathbf{q}=-k\nabla T$ with
$k=\mu c_p/\mathrm{Pr}$. The constant viscosity is
\[
\mu=\frac{\rho_\infty U_\infty L_{\mathrm{ref}}}{\mathrm{Re}},
\]
matching each case Reynolds number.

The case files use $\rho_\infty=1$, $U_\infty=1$,
$L_{\mathrm{ref}}=1$, $\mathrm{Pr}=0.72$, $R=1$, and
$p_\infty=1/(\gamma M_\infty^2)$. Force coefficients are
\[
C_D=\frac{D}{q_\infty A_{\mathrm{ref}}},\qquad
C_L=\frac{L}{q_\infty A_{\mathrm{ref}}},\qquad
q_\infty=\tfrac12\rho_\infty U_\infty^2,
\]
with $A_{\mathrm{ref}}=1$ for both geometries.

\section{Spatial Discretization}
\subsection{Finite-volume residual}
For an owned cell $c$,
\[
\frac{d\mathbf{U}_c}{dt}V_c=-\sum_{f\in\partial c}\bigl(\mathbf{F}_f^i-\mathbf{F}_f^v\bigr)\cdot\mathbf{n}_f A_f,
\]
where $\mathbf{n}_f$ is the unit normal from the left cell to the right cell.
The interior flux is assembled once per face and added with opposite sign to
its two cells, so the scheme is exactly conservative.

\subsection{Second-order reconstruction}
Primitive gradients of $[\rho,u,v,p]$ are computed with an inverse-distance
weighted least-squares fit over face neighbors,
\[
A=\sum_j w_j\,\mathbf{d}_j\mathbf{d}_j^T,\qquad
\mathbf{b}_q=\sum_j w_j\,\mathbf{d}_j(q_j-q_c),\qquad
\nabla q=A^{-1}\mathbf{b}_q,\qquad w_j=|\mathbf{d}_j|^{-2}.
\]
Left and right face states are then
\[
q_L=q_c+\phi_c\nabla q_c\cdot(\mathbf{x}_f-\mathbf{x}_c)
\]
and the corresponding reconstruction for the right cell. Reconstruction and
limiting are active in every production run; a first-order state is used only
as a per-cell positivity fallback when a reconstructed density or pressure is
nonpositive.

\subsection{Limiter and positivity}
The Venkatakrishnan limiter is applied separately to each primitive variable:
\[
\phi=\frac{(\Delta_+^2+\epsilon^2)\Delta_-+2\Delta_-^2\Delta_+}
{\Delta_-\bigl(\Delta_+^2+2\Delta_-^2+\Delta_-\Delta_++\epsilon^2\bigr)},
\qquad
\epsilon^2=(K\sqrt{V_c})^3,
\]
with $K=0.3$. If any reconstructed face state has $\rho\le0$ or $p\le0$,
the face reverts to the cell-center state; the nonlinear updates additionally
halve a proposed step until density and pressure remain positive.

\subsection{Inviscid and viscous fluxes}
Inviscid cases use Roe's flux with a Harten--Yee entropy fix
($\delta=0.1(|u_n|+a)$); laminar cases use Rusanov/LLF with the case-supplied
dissipation scale. The viscous face flux uses averaged velocity and
temperature gradients corrected in the face-normal direction,
$g=g_{\mathrm{avg}}-(g_{\mathrm{avg}}\cdot\mathbf{d}-\Delta q)\mathbf{d}/|\mathbf{d}|^2$,
to form the Newtonian stress and Fourier heat flux above. Wall pressure forces
and tangential wall shear are accumulated separately, so
\texttt{pressure\_drag} and \texttt{viscous\_drag} are never mixed.

\section{Boundary Conditions}
The farfield uses one-dimensional characteristic states ($R^+$ from the
interior, $R^-$ from freestream, entropy and tangential velocity selected by
inflow/outflow direction, with explicit supersonic branches). The inviscid
slip wall applies the reconstructed wall pressure with zero normal velocity;
the no-slip adiabatic wall sets $\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{wall}}=0$ and zero heat
flux, and enforces the wall-normal velocity derivative
$\partial u_n/\partial n=-u_c/d_c$. The surface CSV reports boundary-state
values, not adjacent cell centers: no-slip rows have
$u=v=\mathrm{Mach}=0$, and slip-wall rows keep tangential velocity while the
normal velocity is zero.

\section{Implicit and Transient Time Integration}
Steady cases use local pseudo-time continuation with the supplied CFL ramp
and per-cell spectral radius
\[
\frac{V_c}{\Delta\tau_c}=\frac{\lambda_c+4\lambda_v}{\mathrm{CFL}},
\qquad
\lambda_c=\sum_f A_f(|u_n|+a),\qquad
\lambda_v=\mu_{\mathrm{eff}}\sum_f A_f^2/(\rho V_c).
\]
Each nonlinear step performs the supplied 3--80 (3--100 for the $M=2$
laminar case) inner relaxations, stopping early when the total residual ratio
meets the case target. The inner solve is a symmetric flux-split
Gauss--Seidel/LU-SGS iteration with the upwind Jacobian
$A^\pm=\tfrac12(A_n\pm\rho I)$, area-scaled consistently with the residual.
For MPI runs the sweeps follow a flow-aligned rank ordering and exchange
corrections across halo edges each sweep; a matrix-free, right-preconditioned
GMRES path is also available in source.

For the Reynolds 200 transient the outer loop uses BDF2,
\[
\left(\frac{V}{\Delta\tau}+\frac{3V}{2\Delta t}\right)\delta\mathbf{U}
=-\mathbf{R}-\frac{3\mathbf{U}^{n+1}-4\mathbf{U}^n+\mathbf{U}^{n-1}}{2\Delta t}V,
\]
with $\Delta t=0.01$ and fixed $\mathrm{CFL}=1.0$. $\mathbf{U}^n$ and
$\mathbf{U}^{n-1}$ are frozen during every inner iteration of a physical step
and updated only after that step is accepted. The inner convergence target is
$10^{-3}$ on the total spatial-plus-physical-time residual.

\section{MPI Parallelization and METIS Partitioning}
Rank 0 reads the mesh and calls METIS \texttt{k-way} partitioning on the
cell-adjacency graph. Every rank then receives only its owned cells, its one
layer of ghost cells, and the faces of its owned cells; the full mesh and
full state are never replicated during iterations. Halo data (conservative
states and primitive gradients) is exchanged with neighbor ranks through
nonblocking \texttt{MPI\_Isend}/\texttt{MPI\_Irecv} using symmetric rank-pair
tags. Residual and force norms use \texttt{MPI\_Allreduce}; the implicit
solver exchanges correction vectors across halo edges each sweep. Partition
diagnostics (owned/ghost counts, boundary faces, neighbor lists, send/receive
counts, edge cut, and load balance) are written to every case directory.

\section{Output, Reproducibility, and Sanity Checks}
The build and run commands are documented in \texttt{README.md}. Every case
directory contains \texttt{metadata.json}, partition diagnostics,
\texttt{residuals.csv}, \texttt{forces.csv}, \texttt{surface.csv},
\texttt{field\_final.vtu}, \texttt{restart\_final.*}, \texttt{stdout.log},
and \texttt{run\_status.json}. Figures are regenerated from those files with
\texttt{tools/plot\_histories.py}, \texttt{tools/plot\_surface.py}, and
\texttt{tools/plot\_fields.py}; \texttt{tools/make\_sanity\_checks.py} and
\texttt{tools/make\_manifests.py} regenerate the sanity-check and manifest
artifacts. Sanity checks include finite positive density/pressure, near-zero
lift for symmetric NACA runs, positive cylinder drag, nonzero unsteady lift
for Reynolds 200, wall-velocity and skin-friction checks, and figure-variable
name checks.
