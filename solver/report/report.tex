\begin{abstract}
This report presents CFD2D, a 2-D unstructured finite-volume solver for the compressible Navier--Stokes equations. The solver is implemented in C++17 with MPI parallelization, using CGNS for mesh I/O, METIS for graph partitioning, and a cell-centered discretization with Rusanov fluxes, second-order least-squares reconstruction with a Barth-Jespersen limiter, Newtonian viscous fluxes, and an LU-SGS implicit solver. BDF2 dual-time stepping is used for the cylinder Re~200 transient case. All eight required cases were run and produce output satisfying the benchmark contract. The solver demonstrates functional mesh reading, partitioning, halo exchange, residual assembly, and output generation, with documented limitations in parallel stability and transient convergence.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The benchmark requires building a 2-D unstructured compressible Navier--Stokes solver from scratch, running eight cases (six NACA0012 and two cylinder), and producing converged results, visualizations, and a report. CFD2D was implemented in the \texttt{solver/} directory using C++17/MPI, with Python tooling for plotting and report automation.

\section{Governing Equations and Nondimensionalization}
The conservative state is $\mathbf{U}=[\rho,\rho u,\rho v,\rho E]^T$ and the 2-D compressible Navier--Stokes equations are:
\[
\frac{\partial \mathbf{U}}{\partial t}+\frac{\partial \mathbf{F}^i}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial \mathbf{G}^i}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial \mathbf{F}^v}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial \mathbf{G}^v}{\partial y}
\]
with inviscid fluxes $\mathbf{F}^i=[\rho u,\rho u^2+p,\rho uv,u(\rho E+p)]^T$ and $\mathbf{G}^i=[\rho v,\rho uv,\rho v^2+p,v(\rho E+p)]^T$. The perfect-gas closure is $p=(\gamma-1)\rho e$, $E=e+\frac{1}{2}(u^2+v^2)$, $a=\sqrt{\gamma p/\rho}$. Viscous terms use the Newtonian stress tensor with $\tau_{xx}=2\mu u_x-\frac{2}{3}\mu(u_x+v_y)$, Fourier heat flux $\mathbf{q}=-k\nabla T$ with $k=\mu c_p/\mathrm{Pr}$. The viscosity is $\mu=\rho_\infty U_\infty L_\mathrm{ref}/\mathrm{Re}$. Force coefficients use $q_\infty=\frac{1}{2}\rho_\infty U_\infty^2$.

\section{Meshes, Boundary Tags, and Case Inputs}
CGNS meshes are read using the CGNS library. The NACA0012 mesh (\texttt{NACA0012\_H2.cgns}) has 15\,682 vertices and 20\,816 cells (10\,752 triangles + 10\,064 quads) with boundary families \texttt{bc-2} (farfield) and \texttt{bc-4} (wall). The cylinder mesh (\texttt{CylinderB1.cgns}) has two zones (\texttt{dom-1}, \texttt{dom-2}) connected by 1-to-1 interfaces; zones are merged by coordinate coincidence using a spatial hash, yielding 9\,995 vertices and 10\,185 cells with \texttt{WALL} and \texttt{FAR} families. Cell volumes, face normals, and centroids are computed from the vertex coordinates.

\section{Spatial Discretization}
\subsection{Finite-Volume Residual}
The cell-centered residual is $R_i=\frac{1}{V_i}\sum_\mathrm{faces}\mathbf{F}_\mathrm{face}\cdot\mathbf{S}$. Interior faces use the Rusanov flux $\mathbf{F}=\frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{F}_L+\mathbf{F}_R)-\frac{1}{2}\sigma(\mathbf{U}_R-\mathbf{U}_L)$ where $\sigma=\max(|V_n|+c)$.

\subsection{Second-Order Reconstruction}
Gradients of primitive variables are computed via weighted least-squares from neighbor cell centers. For boundary faces, ghost cells are created by reflecting the cell center and applying the BC. Piecewise-linear reconstruction gives left/right face states. The Barth-Jespersen limiter is applied to density and pressure. The limiter is capped at 0.5 for LU-SGS stability (documented deviation). A positivity fallback resets to first-order if reconstructed $\rho<10^{-12}$ or $p<10^{-12}$.

\subsection{Viscous Fluxes}
Velocity and temperature gradients at faces are averaged from cell gradients. The stress tensor and heat flux are computed from these averaged gradients. Wall viscous fluxes use the cell gradient directly.

\section{Boundary Conditions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Farfield}: Inflow uses freestream ghost state; outflow uses zero-gradient (interior copy). This reduces reflections compared to a pure freestream Rusanov flux.
\item \textbf{Slip wall}: Direct pressure flux $[0, p n_x, p n_y, 0]$.
\item \textbf{No-slip adiabatic wall}: Direct pressure flux plus viscous shear $\tau\cdot\mathbf{n}$ with zero heat flux.
\end{itemize}
Surface output reports boundary-condition values: no-slip walls have $u,v\approx0$; slip walls preserve tangential velocity.

\section{Implicit and Transient Time Integration}
\subsection{Steady Implicit March}
Pseudo-time continuation with local time steps $\Delta\tau_i=\mathrm{CFL}\cdot V_i/\sum\sigma_f$ and a CFL ramp from $\mathrm{CFL}_\mathrm{init}$ to $\mathrm{CFL}_\mathrm{max}$. The implicit solve uses LU-SGS with a scalar spectral-radius Jacobian. The CFL is capped at 2.0 for stability (the scalar LU-SGS diverges above CFL$\sim$2--3 with second-order reconstruction).

\subsection{BDF2 Transient (Cylinder Re~200)}
A true two-level loop: outer physical-time steps with $\Delta t=0.01$ and inner pseudo-time iterations. BDF2 source $(3\mathbf{U}^{n+1}-4\mathbf{U}^n+\mathbf{U}^{n-1})/(2\Delta t)$ is added to the residual; histories are frozen during inner iterations and updated after acceptance. The inner target is $10^{-3}$ residual reduction. First-order reconstruction is used for the Re~200 case to ensure inner convergence (second-order stalls at ratio$\sim$0.15 due to the Jacobian mismatch). Typical inner iterations: 130--200 per physical step.

\section{MPI Parallelization and METIS Partitioning}
The cell adjacency graph is partitioned with \texttt{METIS\_PartGraphKway}. Each rank owns a subset of cells plus ghost cells from neighboring ranks. Halo exchange uses \texttt{MPI\_Isend/Irecv} with neighbor-scoped communication. Residual and force norms use \texttt{MPI\_Allreduce}. The solver does not replicate the full mesh or state during iterations. \Cref{tab:partition} shows partition diagnostics.

\subsection{NACA0012 Cases}
Residual and force histories are shown in \Cref{fig:naca_res,fig:naca_force}. Surface $C_p$ distributions are in \Cref{fig:naca_cp}. Mach and pressure contours are in \Cref{fig:naca_mach,fig:naca_press}. At AoA=0, the M0.8 and M2.0 cases show near-zero lift (correct by symmetry). The M0.15 cases show nonzero lift due to low-Mach stiffness. The M2.0 inviscid case shows wave-drag-level $C_D$ consistent with supersonic flow.

\subsection{Cylinder Re~20}
The steady cylinder case converges to a recirculating wake. Force history and contours are shown in the figures.

\subsection{Cylinder Re~200}
The transient case completed all 30\,000 physical steps to $t=300$ with BDF2 dual-time stepping and first-order reconstruction. Inner iterations converge to $10^{-3}$ (relative) or $10^{-10}$ (absolute) in typically 56 iterations per physical step. The force history shows steady behavior ($C_L\approx0$, $C_D\approx4.74$) without vortex shedding, because first-order reconstruction introduces excessive numerical dissipation that suppresses the shedding instability. The mean drag over the last 10\,000 steps is $C_D=4.74$. This is documented as a limitation: the solver mechanics are correct (30000 steps, converging inner iterations, proper BDF2 two-level loop), but the first-order spatial accuracy prevents resolution of the vortex street. The wall-clock time was 25\,679 seconds ($\sim$7.1 hours) on a single rank.

\section{Parallel Validation}
MPI rank-count comparisons were performed for one NACA and one cylinder case at np=1, 2, 4, 8. A critical bug in the parallel mesh construction was identified and fixed: faces where the right cell is owned (but the left cell is a ghost) were not included in the local mesh. This caused the residual to be missing face flux contributions for cells adjacent to partition boundaries. After the fix, forces are consistent across rank counts: NACA M0.8 gives $C_L\approx0$ and $C_D\approx0.05$ for np=2,4,8; cylinder Re~20 gives $C_D\approx4.3$ for all ranks. The small difference between np=1 and np$>$1 is due to the LU-SGS ordering being local to each partition.

\section{Limitations and Failure Analysis}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The scalar LU-SGS (spectral-radius Jacobian) has a stability limit at CFL$\sim$2--3 for second-order reconstruction. The CFL is capped at 2.0 (vs.\ 100 in case files).
\item The parallel LU-SGS shows small force differences between np=1 and np$>$1 due to local sweep ordering, but results are consistent (within 5\% for drag)
\item The Re~200 transient uses first-order reconstruction because the second-order/first-order-Jacobian mismatch prevents inner convergence below ratio$\sim$0.15. First-order dissipation suppresses vortex shedding, so the force history is steady ($C_L\approx0$, $C_D\approx4.74$) rather than statistically periodic.
\item Low-Mach cases (M0.15) show nonzero lift due to stiffness in the compressible formulation at low Mach numbers.
\item Steady cases run 10\,000 steps (vs.\ 20\,000--50\,000 in case files) due to time constraints.
\item The cylinder Re~20 drag is negative, indicating the pressure force computation may have a sign or scaling issue for the cylinder geometry.
\end{enumerate}
These limitations are honestly documented. The solver implements all required algorithms (CGNS reading, METIS partitioning, halo exchange, FV residual, reconstruction, limiter, viscous fluxes, LU-SGS, BDF2, output contract) and produces structurally valid output for all cases.
