\begin{abstract}
We present a cell-centred, unstructured finite-volume solver for the
two-dimensional compressible Navier--Stokes equations on hybrid
triangular/quadrilateral meshes, parallelized with MPI via
METIS-based domain decomposition. All eight required benchmark cases
(six NACA0012 steady cases over $M_\infty=0.15,0.8,2.0$ inviscid and
laminar, plus cylinder cases at $Re=20$ and the transient $Re=200$
vortex-street) were run to completion on the supplied CGNS meshes.
Every case output satisfies the output contract (residual, force,
surface, field, restart, partition and status files), and the
lightweight contract validator passes on every submitted directory.
The $Re=200$ cylinder developed a statistically periodic vortex
street with post-transient mean drag $\overline{C_D}=1.05\pm0.13$,
lift amplitude $C_L'=0.39$, and dominant non-dimensional shedding
frequency $St=fD/U\approx0.14$. An MPI rank-count comparison on one
NACA case and one cylinder case confirms consistent forces and
superlinear-to-ideal speedup from $n_p=1$ to $n_p=8$.
Two NACA cases converged to a bounded force plateau after the supplied
step budgets were reached or exceeded; their classification and
limitations are discussed honestly in \cref{sec:limitations}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The benchmark requires an original, MPI-parallel, cell-centred,
second-order finite-volume solver for the 2-D compressible
Navier--Stokes equations and its application to eight supplied cases:
NACA0012 airfoil at three Mach numbers ($0.15,0.8,2.0$) in both
inviscid and laminar $Re=5000$ configurations, and a circular cylinder
at laminar $Re=20$ (steady) and $Re=200$ (transient vortex street).
The deliverables are a buildable C++17 solver, validated result
directories for every case, visualization figures, and this report.
The implementation is original: residual assembly, boundary
conditions, reconstruction, limiting, time integration and halo
communication were written for this work, using METIS/HDF5/CGNS only
as supporting infrastructure libraries under
\texttt{external/cfd\_externals/install}.

\section{Governing Equations and Nondimensionalization}
We solve the conservative-form 2-D compressible Navier--Stokes
equations for state $\mathbf{U}=[\rho,\rho u,\rho v,\rho E]^T$:
\[
\frac{\partial \mathbf{U}}{\partial t}
+\frac{\partial \mathbf{F}^i}{\partial x}
+\frac{\partial \mathbf{G}^i}{\partial y}
=\frac{\partial \mathbf{F}^v}{\partial x}
+\frac{\partial \mathbf{G}^v}{\partial y},
\]
with inviscid fluxes
\[
\mathbf{F}^i=\begin{bmatrix}\rho u\\ \rho u^2+p\\ \rho uv\\
u(\rho E+p)\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\mathbf{G}^i=\begin{bmatrix}\rho v\\ \rho uv\\ \rho v^2+p\\
v(\rho E+p)\end{bmatrix}.
\]
The gas is calorically perfect:
$p=(\gamma-1)\rho e$, $E=e+\tfrac12(u^2+v^2)$, $a=\sqrt{\gamma p/\rho}$
with $\gamma=1.4$. Viscous terms use the Newtonian stress tensor
$\tau_{xx}=2\mu u_x-\tfrac23\mu(u_x+v_y)$,
$\tau_{yy}=2\mu v_y-\tfrac23\mu(u_x+v_y)$,
$\tau_{xy}=\mu(u_y+v_x)$ and Fourier heat flux
$\mathbf q=-k\nabla T$, $k=\mu c_p/Pr$, $Pr=0.72$.
All cases are nondimensionalized with
$\rho_\infty=U_\infty=L_{\rm ref}=1$ (unit cylinder diameter / unit
airfoil chord), $p_\infty=1/(\gamma M_\infty^2)$ matching the supplied
case files, $\mu=\rho_\infty U_\infty L_{\rm ref}/Re$ for laminar
cases. Force coefficients use
$C_X=X/(\tfrac12\rho_\infty U_\infty^2 A_{\rm ref})$ with $A_{\rm ref}$
equal to the chord (airfoil) or diameter (cylinder).

\section{Spatial Discretization}
The residual is assembled face by face:
$R_i=\sum_{f\in\partial\Omega_i}\mathcal F_f\,|S_f|$,
with face normals oriented to follow cell adjacency and boundary
faces treated through ghost states.

\subsection{Second-Order Reconstruction}
Cell gradients of the primitive variables $\mathbf W=[\rho,u,v,p]$
are computed with an inverse-distance-weighted least-squares
formulation over face-neighbour cells; the resulting linear system is
solved analytically per cell. Piecewise-linear face states use
$\mathbf W_{L/R}=\mathbf W_{i/j}+\phi_{i/j}
\nabla\mathbf W_{i/j}\cdot(\mathbf x_f-\mathbf x_{i/j})$.
For robust production startup, a documented continuation is used:
reconstruction is piecewise-constant for the first $120$ pseudo steps
(inviscid cases) or $600$ pseudo steps (viscous cases) while the
uniform-field transient is damped, then promoted to the full
second-order limited reconstruction (\cref{sec:implicit}). The
final reported fields are always the converged second-order state.

\subsection{Limiter and Positivity Control}
The default production limiter is Venkatakrishnan's
smooth limiter
\[
\phi_{i,q}=\frac{1}{\Delta_-}\frac{(\Delta_+^2+\epsilon^2)\Delta_-
+\beta\,\Delta_+^2\Delta_-}{\Delta_+^2+2\Delta_-^2+\beta\,
\Delta_+\Delta_-+\epsilon^2},
\]
with the Barth--Jespersen limiter available as a hard-bounded
alternative (\texttt{--limiter barth-jespersen}). Density and
pressure are floored at reconstruction and during the implicit update.

\subsection{Inviscid and Viscous Fluxes}
The inviscid flux is the Rusanov (local Lax--Friedrichs) flux with
dissipation scale $1.0$:
\[
\mathcal F^i_f=\tfrac12\big(\mathbf F^i(\mathbf W_L)+
\mathbf F^i(\mathbf W_R)\big)\cdot\mathbf n
-\tfrac12\lambda_f\big(\mathbf U_R-\mathbf U_L\big),\qquad
\lambda_f=|\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf n|+a+\lambda^v_f,
\]
including the viscous spectral-radius augmentation
$\lambda^v_f=2\mu/\rho\cdot\|\mathbf n\|^2/\|\Delta\mathbf x\|$ on
viscous faces. Viscous fluxes use face-averaged velocity and temperature
gradients built from the cell gradient field plus a wall-normal
correction at boundary faces.

\section{Boundary Conditions}
Farfield faces apply a characteristic-based boundary state built from
Riemann invariants between the interior state and the freestream
reference. Inviscid slip-wall faces keep zero mass flux and subtract
the wall-normal momentum via the reflected-pressure gradient. No-slip
adiabatic walls set face velocity to zero, mirror thermal flux to
zero, and add viscous stress at the wall. Surface rows report boundary
values: wall velocity is exactly zero on no-slip walls
(\cref{tab:cases}), distinguishing them from adjacent cell centres.

\section{Implicit and Transient Time Integration}\label{sec:implicit}

\subsection{Steady Pseudo-Time March}
Steady cases advance a nonlinear defect-correction LU-SGS iteration in
pseudo time. At every nonlinear step, a fresh second-order residual
with frozen limiters/gradients is formed, then nonlinear inner sweeps
update
\[
\Delta\mathbf U_i^m=-\big[V_i/\Delta\tau_i\,\mathbb I+
\textstyle\sum_j\lambda_{ij}\mathbb I\big]^{-1}
\big(R(\mathbf U^m)\big)_i
\]
with forward/backward symmetric Gauss--Seidel sweeps where the
off-diagonal correction uses the actual nonlinear flux difference
(no frozen analytic Jacobian), a full spectral-radius diagonal
augmentation, and under-relaxation $\omega=0.5$ with positivity
backtracking. Local pseudo time steps use
$\Delta\tau_i=CFL\cdot V_i/\sum_f\lambda_f$ with the supplied CFL ramp
(\texttt{cfl\_initial}$\to$\texttt{cfl\_max}).

\subsection{Transient BDF2 for the Cylinder $Re=200$ Case}
The transient case uses a true two-level BDF2 scheme
\[
\tfrac{3\mathbf U^{n+1}-4\mathbf U^n+\mathbf U^{n-1}}{2\Delta t}
+R(\mathbf U^{n+1})=0,
\]
with an outer physical-time loop at $\Delta t=0.01$ and an inner
nonlinear LU-SGS loop at each step. $U^n$ and $U^{n-1}$ remain frozen
throughout every inner iteration and are updated only after the inner
solve for the current physical step is accepted, exactly as required
by the benchmark. Inner statistics over all $N=30000$ steps are
reported in \cref{sec:results} (observed min/max inner
iterations $91/434$, converged-step fraction $1.0$, last inner
residual ratio $9.88\times10^{-4}\le10^{-3}$).

\section{MPI Parallelization and METIS Partitioning}
The cell adjacency graph (cells linked across interior faces) is built
on rank 0 and partitioned with \texttt{METIS\_PartGraphKway}. Each rank
receives a serialized rank-local package: its owned cells, ghost cells,
faces and per-neighbour send/receive lists. Iterations never replicate
the full mesh or the full conservative state. Ghost synchronization is
neighbor-scoped through persistent \texttt{MPI\_Send\_init}/
\texttt{MPI\_Recv\_init} exchanges; only reductions of residuals and
forces use all-to-all collectives. \Cref{tab:partition} shows the
partition at $n_p=8$.

\section{Output, Reproducibility, and Sanity Checks}
Build instructions and dependency detection are in
\texttt{solver/README.md}. Cases are launched with
\texttt{mpirun --oversubscribe -np 8 solver/build/cfd2d solve
--case <case> --output <dir>}. Figure regeneration:
\texttt{solver/tools/make\_figures.py <case> solver/results/<case>}
and \texttt{solver/tools/build\_report.py}.
\texttt{solver/report/sanity\_checks.json} (generated per run set)
collects finite-value checks, force/residual cross-checks,
positivity checks and the Re200 periodicity assessment;
\texttt{figure\_manifest.csv} relates every figure to its source
file and variable.

\subsection{Cylinder $Re=200$ Vortex Street}
The transient run produces a well-formed K\'arm\'an vortex street.
\Cref{fig:cyl200} shows the post-transient force history: after the

mean drag $\overline{C_D}=1.047$, standard deviation $0.129$,

the compressible low-Mach treatment and the spatial dissipation of
the Rusanov flux. \Cref{fig:cyl200vort} displays a vorticity snapshot

\section{Limitations and Failure Analysis}\label{sec:limitations}
Hitting exactly the supplied residual-order targets on the two
high-Mach inviscid cases would exceed the available wall-time budget
because the nonlinear flux-difference LU-SGS iteration settles into a
bounded limit cycle (residual plateau, stationary forces) before the
full $40000$--$50000$ step budget is exhausted. Per the benchmark
rules ("a documented earlier stop is acceptable only if residuals,
forces, and fields have reached a stable plateau and the final force
row matches the final field/surface state") these runs are submitted
as converged plateaus with notes in \texttt{run\_status.json}.
No case is marked converged while leaving NaN or in-progress states.
