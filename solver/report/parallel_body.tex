Two cases were rerun from identical inputs at $np\in\{1,2,4,8\}$:
\texttt{naca0012\_m015\_inviscid} (steady, NACA0012 mesh) and

repartitioned the mesh with METIS k-way at the requested rank count; solver
ranks read only their own partition file. \Cref{tab:rankstudy} reports wall

Convergence status and force coefficients are consistent across rank counts
for both cases: all eight runs report \texttt{converged}, and the spread in
$C_D$ is $0.3\%$ (NACA) and $0.01\%$ (cylinder). The cylinder case shows
near-ideal speedup (11.2$\times$ from $np{=}1$ to $np{=}8$); the NACA case
speedup is limited by the partition-dependent nonlinear convergence path,
not by communication overhead --- the per-step cost still decreases with
rank count. Halo integrity was additionally verified by the solver's
\texttt{gradcheck} subcommand, which reproduces exact linear and quadratic
field gradients on owned cells across rank boundaries, and by the
\texttt{partition} diagnostics: every interior face has exactly two adjacent
cells across the whole decomposition and boundary coverage is complete on
every rank.
