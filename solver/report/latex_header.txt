\section{Governing Equations and Nondimensionalization}
The solver integrates the 2D compressible Navier--Stokes equations:
\[\frac{\partial \mathbf{U}}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\mathbf{F}^c - \mathbf{F}^v) = 0\]
where $\mathbf{U} = [\rho, \rho u, \rho v, \rho E]^T$.
EOS:  = (\gamma-1)(\rho E - \frac{1}{2}\rho|\mathbf{u}|^2)$, $\gamma=1.4$.
Variables nondimensionalized by $\rho_\infty$, \infty$, =1$.
\section{Numerical Methods}
\subsection{Inviscid Flux}
Rusanov (Local Lax-Friedrichs) scheme. Turkel preconditioning for <0.3$, \geq 100$ viscous cases.
\subsection{Viscous Flux}
Average cell-gradient Green--Gauss method. Newtonian stress and Fourier heat flux.
\subsection{Reconstruction}
Piecewise-linear least-squares with Barth--Jespersen limiter.
\subsection{Time Integration}
Steady: pseudo-time LU-SGS with local time stepping, CFL ramp.
Transient (Re~200): BDF2, $\Delta t=0.01$, frozen-history inner LU-SGS, tolerance 0^{-3}$.
\subsection{MPI}
METIS k-way partitioning. Non-blocking halo exchange. MPI\_Allreduce for reductions.
\section{Boundary Conditions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Farfield:} Riemann-based inflow/outflow.
\item \textbf{Slip wall:} Zero normal velocity, zero-gradient pressure.
\item \textbf{No-slip adiabatic wall:} $\mathbf{u}=0$, $\partial T/\partial n=0$.
\end{itemize}
