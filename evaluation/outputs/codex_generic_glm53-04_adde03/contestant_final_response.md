- **Solver** (`src/`): C++17/MPI cell-centered FVM on unstructured CGNS meshes with multi-zone stitching (the two-zone cylinder mesh merges into one conforming mesh). METIS k-way partitioning scatters rank-local owned+ghost meshes; iterations use neighbor-scoped Isend/Irecv halo exchange only — no full mesh or state replication. Roe flux with a Harten entropy fix on the acoustic waves only (contacts/shear stay clean), Green-Gauss gradients, Venkatakrishnan limiter, Newtonian/Fourier viscous fluxes with case-matched viscosity, and an LU-SGS implicit solver with full Roe-Jacobian off-diagonal blocks.
